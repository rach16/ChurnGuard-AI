Return your findings as claims. Each claim is one or two sentences and cites the refs of the tool results it relies on. Copy numbers, amounts, dates and names exactly as the tools gave them. Do not add, average, count or rank. If you could not find something, make that a claim with no numbers and no citations.
A claim with no citation may only say what was not found or not available. Never claim that something does not exist or was never mentioned: a search returns a sample. Do not write refs like (T1) inside the text; put them in citations.
